= Reading Assignment 2

== Objective
The assignment's goal is to get you deeper into terms concerning html and to make you familiar with basic terms of Cascading Style Sheets (css).

== Materials

. https://www.w3schools.com/html/html_elements.asp[HTML elements] - Be careful that the information about "empty elements" is not according to the spec. Following the W3C specification we call them _void elements_.

. https://www.w3schools.com/html/html_headings.asp[Headings]
. https://www.w3schools.com/html/html_paragraphs.asp[Paragraphs] - Be careful when using the `<br>` element. Doing line breaks in this style should be only at very rare occasions. Most of the time it is clearer to start a new paragraph with the text you want to have separated by a new line.

To prepare and check your own level of knowledge answer these questions.

- Extra information in the opening tag (scr)

-<!DOCTYPE html>

** Article (`<article>`) = <artictle>Hello</article>

** line break (`<br>`) <br>
** Headings (`<h1>`, ...) <h1>hello world</h1>

** Section (`<section>`) <section>Content</section>
** Label appearing in the title bar (`<title>`) <title>My Website</title>
** Link to other pages (`<a>`) <a href="page.html">Linkk</a>

** Head with title and meta elements

=== CSS
. Name the components of a css rule properly
- Selector, declaration block, property and value
. Use combinators properly: direct child, descendant, adjacent sibling, sibling
- div > p, div p, h1 + p, h1 ~ p 
. Specify colors by color names and in hexadecimal notation
- red, blue, #FF0000, #0000FF
. Declarations for color, background color
- color: red, background-color: yellow;
. Pseudo classes for the `<a>` element
- :link, :visited, :hover, :active
. Declaration for background image
- background-image: url("image.jpg");
. Declaration for text alignment (left, right, center, justify)
- text-align: left|right|center|justify;
. Declaration for text decoration (overline, underline, line-through)
- text-decoration: overline|underline|line-through;
. Declaration for text transformation (uppercase, lowercase, capitalize)
- text-transform: uppercase|lowercase|capitalize;
. Identify the difference between serif and sans-serif fonts
- Serif haas small strokes; sans-serif has no small strokes
. Declarations for font families
- font-family: Arial, sans-serif;
. Declarations for font style normal and italic
- font-style: normal|italic;
. Declarations for font sizes
- font-size: 15px|1em;
. Declarations for font weights
- font-weight: normal|bold;
